About AI Doctor

AI Doctor is an intelligent healthcare assistant designed to provide instant symptom analysis, preliminary health guidance, and medical information using Artificial Intelligence. The system helps users understand potential health conditions quickly while promoting informed healthcare decisions.

What is AI Doctor?

AI Doctor is a smart healthcare platform that leverages machine learning and artificial intelligence to assist users in understanding symptoms, obtaining health-related recommendations, and accessing medical information. It acts as a virtual health companion available 24/7, helping users receive quick guidance before consulting healthcare professionals.
